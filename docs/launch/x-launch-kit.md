## First posts (drafts; publish only after the account is created)

### 1 — Intro

Electricity is an asset. The question is what to do with each kWh.

SYNPORA compares Bitcoin mining, AI compute, batteries and grid export in one decision workflow — with transparent assumptions and no automatic hardware control.

Explore the demo: https://synpora.com

### 2 — The problem

A profitable ASIC on paper can become a bad use of power when electricity prices, hashprice, uptime or hardware efficiency change.

Energy decisions need current inputs, explicit assumptions and scenarios — not a single static ROI number.

That’s what we’re building at SYNPORA.

### 3 — Product principles

Three principles behind SYNPORA:
1. Make assumptions visible.
2. Compare alternatives on a consistent €/kWh basis.
3. Keep operational decisions under human control.

Decision support first. Automation is not enabled by default.

### 4 — What-if thinking

What changes if your electricity price rises by €0.03/kWh? If AI utilization drops? If mining revenue changes?

A useful decision tool should make those trade-offs easy to test before hardware or capital is committed.

SYNPORA: https://synpora.com

### 5 — Pilot recruitment

We’re preparing a small SYNPORA pilot for miners and compute operators.

We’re looking for people willing to sanity-check the assumptions, compare outputs against their own operating data, and tell us where the workflow is useful — or wrong.

Interested? Reply or message us. Please do not post credentials or sensitive site data publicly.

## Two-week publishing cadence

- Week 1: publish intro, principles, one educational mining-economics post, then a demo walkthrough.
- Week 2: publish a worked scenario with clearly labelled illustrative inputs, a pilot invitation, and a product update.
- Aim for 3 useful posts per week plus thoughtful replies to relevant mining/compute discussions.
- Avoid buying followers, mass DMs, engagement bait, and unsupported profit or savings claims.
- Do not post private customer telemetry, secrets, account details, or unverified performance claims.

## Website launch polish checklist

Before sending paid traffic:
- Remove customer-facing references to a Railway bootstrap password; use a secure owner setup flow or neutral, safe instructions.
- Verify the first-time owner/account setup, login, password reset, and email verification flow end to end.
- Make the demo's first screen show meaningful example values or a clear one-click demo state instead of unexplained dashes.
- Explain when values are illustrative versus live, show data freshness/source and confidence where applicable.
- Test the complete path on mobile: landing page → demo → simulation → pilot/contact or registration.
- Confirm telemetry and recommendation pages never imply hardware writes or autonomous control.
- Add privacy/terms/contact information and basic privacy-conscious analytics before paid campaigns.
- Use an explicit site electricity tariff for site-specific recommendations; do not silently infer one from incomplete historical snapshots.

## Launch gates

1. Public demo works without registration.
2. Account onboarding is safe and tested.
3. Simulation output is understandable and clearly labels data provenance.
4. At least 5–10 pilot users have been invited; feedback and issues are tracked.
5. Paid promotion only after conversion tracking and a small, capped test budget are ready.
